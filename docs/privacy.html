Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026. Plain-language terms for Mamachi Ads. Have a lawyer review them for your country before relying on them.

What we collect

  • Your name or company, email address, and the details of the ad you submit, including the places you want to reach and any note you write for the ad.
  • Your order details and a PayPal payment reference. We do not receive your card number.
  • A private tracking code for your order.
  • If you choose to connect your own social accounts: the connection details you enter, stored encrypted.
  • Messages you send through the contact form.
  • Click counts on the tracked link in your ad, including the country and city the clicks come from when our link service provides them. This does not identify individual people.

Why we use it

To take payment, review and publish your ad, email you after each post, show you your coverage, reach and results, refund you if needed, answer your questions, and learn which writing styles work best.

Who processes it for us

PayPal (all payments), Supabase (database), Cloudflare (our payment server), Google (email sending and AI writing), other AI providers we may use to write ads, Telegram (alerts to us), Short.io (link tracking) and GitHub (website hosting). They handle data only to provide their service to us. Text from your brief may be sent to AI providers to write your ad.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for our accounts and your tracking page. Connected-account details are deleted when your campaign ends or you disconnect. You can ask us to delete your other data through the contact form, except records we must keep by law.

Cookies

We do not set cookies on our own pages. PayPal sets its own at checkout.

Your rights and contact

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Use the contact form on the home page.
